Hi, I'm Katie Lynn, a University of Colorado Boulder graduate with a degree in Information Science and a minor in Creative Technology & Design. My work sits at the intersection of people, technology, data, and design. I love solving problems, learning how people use technology, and making complicated information feel clear and approachable.

My background includes user-centered design, data visualization, research, customer-facing work, and project coordination. I'm a strong communicator, a creative problem-solver, and someone who genuinely enjoys helping people. I’m comfortable learning new systems, keeping a lot of moving pieces organized, and taking ownership from start to finish.

I've also spent the last decade as a photographer, which taught me a lot about communication, relationship-building, creativity, and attention to detail. I’m shifting my professional focus, but I’m still selectively available for photography projects.

Outside of work, you’ll usually find me outside with my cattle dogs, Billie and Frankie, off-roading, traveling, or dreaming about owning a ranch someday. I’ve always loved animals, horses, cattle, and ranch life, and I’m especially excited by work that combines people, technology, and a meaningful real-world impact. I’m based in Boulder—let’s connect!
