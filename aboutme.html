Hi, I'm Katie Lynn, a soon-to-be graduate of the University of Colorado Boulder with a degree in Information Science and a minor in Creative Technology & Design. My work lives at the intersection of data, design, and storytelling. I'm especially drawn to UX/UI design and the creative thinking behind it: how interfaces feel, how information flows, and how design can be both functional and genuinely beautiful.

Data visualization is another space I love, particularly when it goes beyond charts and becomes something worth looking at. I enjoy finding ways to make complex information accessible and visually compelling.

I've spent the last decade as a photographer, and while I'm shifting my focus professionally, I'm still selectively available for shoots. I'm picky about what I take on these days, but if you have something in mind, feel free to reach out - I'm always open to a conversation.

When I'm not designing or buried in a project, you'll find me somewhere outside with my cattle dogs Billie & Frankie, off-roading, or planning the next trip somewhere far away. I'm based in Boulder and love to travel. Let's connect!
